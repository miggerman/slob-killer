# Slob Killer

A simple tracker for food, workouts, water, sleep and groceries that runs in your phone's browser.

**Open it:** https://miggerman.github.io/slob-killer/

On iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. Always open it from that icon so your log stays in one place.

- Type or say what you ate ("2 eggs, a banana and 150 g chicken breast") and check the matches before adding.
- Built-in nutrition for about 120 common foods (USDA averages), plus online search of packaged foods through Open Food Facts.
- Your log is stored only on your own device. Use **Settings → Save a backup** to keep a copy or move to a new phone.
